Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Türkçe · English · Deutsch

Vitesse is a personal app for tracking your vehicle's fuel fill-ups and maintenance events. Your privacy is our priority.

Data Collected

Your fill-up records, maintenance events, vehicle information, and preferences are stored on your device. Vitesse does not create user accounts, sign in with Google accounts, or send your data to its own servers or an automatic cloud backup.

Location Data

The app requests location permission for the “Nearby Stations” feature. To open this feature quickly, your most recent location is stored locally on your device and is used only while location permission is active. Your coordinates are shared with the services listed below for station search, routing, and map display.

The stored location is cleared by the “Delete All Data” action or when location permission is removed. Location permission is not needed if you do not use this feature.

Data Sharing

Your data is not used for advertising or sold to third parties. You are responsible for protecting copies that you export as JSON or Excel files.

Third-Party Services

Overpass API (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems): Receives your coordinates to search for nearby fuel stations.

OSRM (router.project-osrm.org): Receives start and destination coordinates to draw a route to the selected station.

CartoDB (basemaps.cartocdn.com): Provides background map tiles.

Google Maps: Receives the selected station's coordinates for directions only if you press the “Maps” button.

These providers may process network data such as your IP address and request details under their own privacy policies when you connect to their services.

Leaflet and the fonts are bundled with the app and are not loaded from an external content delivery network or Google Fonts.

Notifications

The app requests notification permission for calendar events and maintenance reminders. Notifications are generated locally on your device only. You can disable this permission in your device settings at any time.

Data Deletion

“Reset Data” deletes fill-up and calendar records while keeping vehicle information and preferences. “Delete All Data” clears Vitesse data from the device. Files you previously exported as JSON or Excel remain outside the app and must be deleted separately.

Vitesse does not create user accounts or store your data on its own servers. Therefore, we cannot remotely delete data stored on your device. If you cannot access the app, you can clear Vitesse's storage from Android settings. Our email address is for support only.

Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from that age group.
